# Brand assets

Longshot logo and wordmark, imported directly by the app and bundled from here:

- `longshot-wordmark.png`, `longshot-wordmark-dark.png` — the cursive wordmark (light/dark)
- `longshot-icon-512.png`, `longshot-icon-512_light.png` — app icon (favicon at large sizes, Apple touch icon)
- `longshot-favicon-32.png`, `longshot-favicon-16.png` — small favicons

**These assets are excluded from the repository's MIT License** and may not be
used to brand forks or derived products. See [TRADEMARKS.md](../TRADEMARKS.md).

Forks: replace these files and the values in `src/config/brand.ts` with your own.
